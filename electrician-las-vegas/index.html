A short checklist before you schedule

A little preparation can make the first conversation more useful. Gather what you can, but don’t open electrical equipment or attempt a repair to answer these questions.

  • Note the location of the issue, such as a particular room, outlet, switch or light.
  • Write down any changes you’ve noticed, including flickering lights or a breaker that trips repeatedly.
  • For an installation, list the equipment or fixtures you plan to add and where you want them.
  • Mention whether the work is at a home or a commercial property.
  • Ask what details the electrician needs to prepare an estimate and discuss the proposed scope.

You don’t need to know the technical cause. Accurate observations are more useful than a guess about what’s behind a wall or inside a panel.

Questions worth asking before work begins

A good conversation should leave you with a clear idea of what is being considered. Ask what the work includes, which parts of the electrical system may be involved, and whether anything about your request needs to be reviewed on site. If your project involves a panel or subpanel upgrade, wiring, outlets, switches, lighting or surge protection, mention that when you first get in touch.

For an EV charging setup, describe the charger you have or are considering and where you would like it installed. Big Red Electric Company handles Level 2 EV charger installation. Sharing those details early helps the team understand the project before discussing next steps.

When to call a professional

Contact an electrician when you need new wiring, an electrical installation, changes to a panel, or help with a recurring issue you can’t safely resolve. Damaged wiring, a hot outlet, repeated breaker trips or a burning smell are reasons to stop using the affected equipment and seek professional guidance. Don’t remove a panel cover or work on wiring yourself to investigate.
