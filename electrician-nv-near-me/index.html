A simple checklist for your project

Before you call, gather a few details that can help the first conversation go smoothly:

  • Write down what the project involves and what result you’re hoping for.
  • Note when an electrical issue began and whether it happens every time or only now and then.
  • Identify the area of the home and any equipment or appliances connected to the issue.
  • For planned work, share any remodel or installation dates you’re working around.
  • If you’re considering an EV charger, have the vehicle and charger information available if you have it.

You don’t need to know the technical answer. A clear description is more useful than a guess about which wire, breaker or component may be involved.
